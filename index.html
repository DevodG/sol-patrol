SOL PATROL

Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

Spend the sunshine. Survive the night.

A sol is a Mars day, and the Sun is your battery. A Mars outpost game for ages 7–12, powered by live NASA DONKI space weather. Your crew: Nova and Pip.

You are the Commander of a Mars base. Keep your crew breathing, fed and safe for 8 sols (Mars days). Every choice costs power — choose like a real NASA commander!

Mars: messages home take ~12 minutes. Dust storms block sunshine!

1. Spend sunshine power each day 2. Get ready for tonight 3. Survive 8 sols & earn Science Stars

Uses real NASA Sun data (DONKI) · Free · No download · No account · Best: 0 ⭐

How to Play

  1. Morning: Sunshine gives you Power. Read the Sun News and tonight's forecast.
  2. Your turn: Tap mission cards to run your base. Each card costs Power. You cannot do everything — that is the real astronaut challenge!
  3. Night: Your crew breathes air and eats food. Tonight's event happens. Shields protect you from solar storms.
  4. Win: Keep Air, Food and Crew Health above zero for 8 sols. Earn Science Stars for a better rank!

Word bank: Sol = one Mars day (24h 37m). Radiation = invisible energy from the Sun that can hurt people. Shield = protection that blocks radiation.

Real NASA Science in This Game

  • Live Sun news — DONKI (NASA Open APIs, api.nasa.gov): the game asks NASA for real space-weather events (geomagnetic storms GST + solar energetic particles SEP) from the last 30 days. If the internet is off, it shows archived real events and says so. It never pretends old data is live.
  • Radiation — RadLab / OSDR (NASA Open Science Data Repository): real space-station radiation is about 0.5 mSv a day, about 50 times your daily dose on Earth. That is why shields matter.
  • Crew health — OSDR astronaut research: NASA studies sleep, bones and feelings in space. Rest and Call Earth cards come from that science.
  • Mars place — Mars Trek & Mars missions: our base sits in Jezero Plains, where NASA's Perseverance rover explores. Rover ice and dust events use real Mars facts.
  • Food in space — CSA & NASA Veggie: growing food uses water and air, just like the Grow Food card.

Data links: api.nasa.gov · science.nasa.gov (OSDR/RadLab) · mars.nasa.gov (Mars Trek). Game numbers are simplified for kids, facts are real.
